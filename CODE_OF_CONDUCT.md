# Code of Conduct

ZapDL is a welcoming project for contributors and users of every background, language and level of experience.

## Our standards

Expected:
- Be respectful, patient and constructive, including in code review.
- Assume good faith and ask clarifying questions.
- Give credit and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect that contributors write and read many languages, including right-to-left ones.

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block contributors who violate this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers via the repository's contact options. Reports are handled confidentially.
